Campus photos for the login page
================================

The two photos on the login page live in this folder:

  campus-1.jpg   top card     (caption: "Placement drives")
  campus-2.jpg   bottom card  (caption: "Campus to corporate")

To change a photo, replace the file and keep the same name.
.jpeg, .png and .webp also work. Use photos you have permission to use.

Tips
- At least 800 pixels wide gives sharp results; under 400 KB each keeps the page fast.
  (The current two were cut from a small reference picture, so they look a little soft.)
- The card crops the photo to fit, so keep people near the middle.
  If faces get cut off, set "position" for that photo in
  src/config/loginShowcase.js, for example position: 'center 30%'.
- Captions and alt text are in the same file.

If a photo is removed, a designed navy tile with an icon is shown instead.
Restart "npm run dev" if a new photo does not appear straight away.
